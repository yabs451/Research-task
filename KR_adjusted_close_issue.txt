================================================================================
KR (KROGER) ADJUSTED-CLOSE ANOMALY IN DATASET D — INVESTIGATION REPORT
Investigated 2026-08-08. Investigation and documentation only: no data, code or
existing result was modified, corrected or regenerated.
================================================================================

SUMMARY OF THE FINDING

During Dataset D acquisition, KR was the only asset of 100 to fail the
Dataset A identity check (adjusted-close return correlation 0.944, below the
0.98 threshold). The cause is now established conclusively:

  EODHD's dividend feed contains a $6.85 "dividend" on KR.US dated 2022-10-21
  that Kroger never paid. It is the ALBERTSONS (ACI) special dividend, declared
  on 2022-10-14 as part of the Kroger-Albertsons merger agreement, misattributed
  to the acquirer's ticker. EODHD applies it when building adjusted_close,
  which corrupts KR's adjusted-price series.

The Dataset D download itself is correct. The raw open/high/low/close/volume
data is sound and matches every independent source. Only the provider's
adjusted_close column is affected, and only in a specific, well-understood way.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DOWNLOAD AND ALIGNMENT INTEGRITY — NO ACQUISITION ERROR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

KR rows in data/raw/dataset_d_eodhd/dataset_d_ohlcv.csv:

  rows                          3,547
  identifier used               KR.US (single identifier, no fallback)
  date range                    2009-01-02 .. 2023-02-03
  dates sorted ascending        yes
  duplicate dates               0
  missing values in OHLCV       0
  rows with high < low          0
  rows with close outside [low, high]   0
  rows with open outside [low, high]    0
  non-positive prices           0

There is no duplication, no gap and no row misalignment anywhere, and
specifically none around 2022-10-21. Every row is internally consistent, which
rules out a shifted or mis-joined series.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  date         open    high    low    close   adjusted_close   adj/close   close ret   adj ret
  2022-10-17   43.31   43.48   41.82   42.11    32.2524        0.765908     -2.433%    -2.433%
  2022-10-18   42.57   43.32   42.36   43.16    33.0566        0.765908     +2.493%    +2.493%
  2022-10-19   43.28   43.66   42.91   42.94    32.8881        0.765908     -0.510%    -0.510%

  2022-10-21   42.34   43.97   42.15   43.63    39.8974        0.914449     +3.462%   +23.528%  <--
  2022-10-24   43.73   44.17   42.85   43.99    40.2266        0.914449     +0.825%    +0.825%

The OHLC values on 2022-10-21 describe an entirely ordinary trading day: the
stock opened at 42.34, traded between 42.15 and 43.97, and closed at 43.63 -
a +3.46% move from the previous close of 42.17. Nothing in the raw price data
is unusual.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW THE ~+23.5% RETURN ARISES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The reported figure is the ordinary one-day percentage change of the
adjusted_close column:

    (39.8974 / 32.2983) - 1 = +0.23528  =  +23.528%

By contrast the raw-close return over the same two dates is:

    (43.63 / 42.17) - 1 = +0.03462  =  +3.462%

The discrepancy is not a return calculation error. It arises because the two
adjusted_close values on either side of 2022-10-21 are expressed on DIFFERENT
adjustment bases, so dividing one by the other mixes a price move with a change
in the adjustment factor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE ADJUSTMENT FACTOR AND ITS DISCONTINUITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The ratio adjusted_close / close is the cumulative adjustment applied to a given
date for all subsequent corporate actions. It should rise in small steps, one
per ex-dividend date, and jump only on splits.

For KR it is exactly constant at 0.765908 through 2022-10-20 and exactly
constant at 0.914449 from 2022-10-21 onward - a single discontinuous step of
+19.394%.

Every other adjustment step in KR's 2009-2023 history is consistent with a real
corporate action:

  date          ratio step     implied $/share     event
  2021-02-11      +0.538%          0.180           quarterly dividend
  2021-05-13      +0.494%          0.182           quarterly dividend
  2021-08-12      +0.491%          0.209           quarterly dividend
  2021-11-12      +0.497%          0.211           quarterly dividend
  2022-02-14      +0.456%          0.208           quarterly dividend
  2022-05-12      +0.388%          0.210           quarterly dividend
  2022-08-12      +0.554%          0.262           quarterly dividend
  2022-10-21     +19.394%          8.462           <-- no matching action
  2022-11-14      +0.544%          0.253           quarterly dividend
  2015-07-14     +99.9998%           n/a           2-for-1 stock split (genuine)

Across the full history there are 58 adjustment steps; the median is +0.46% and
the 95th percentile +0.62%. Apart from the genuine 2015 split, the 2022-10-21
step is more than thirty times larger than any other.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE CAUSE, CONFIRMED FROM EODHD'S OWN CORPORATE-ACTION FEED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EODHD's dividend record for KR.US in 2022 (read-only query, nothing stored):

  2022-02-14   0.21   Quarterly   declared 2022-01-21   paid 2022-03-01
  2022-05-12   0.21   Quarterly   declared 2022-03-10   paid 2022-06-01
  2022-08-12   0.26   Quarterly   declared 2022-06-23   paid 2022-09-01
  2022-10-21   6.85   period=None declared=None  record=None  paid=None   <--
  2022-11-14   0.26   Quarterly   declared 2022-09-15   paid 2022-12-01

The 2022-10-21 entry is the only one with no period, no declaration date, no
record date and no payment date - it does not look like a normal dividend
record.

The same query for ACI.US (Albertsons) returns:

  2022-10-21   6.85   period='Other'   declared 2022-10-14   paid 2023-01-20
  2022-10-28   0.12   Quarterly        declared 2022-10-18   paid 2022-11-14

Identical amount, identical date. Albertsons' $6.85 special dividend was
declared on 2022-10-14 - the day Kroger and Albertsons announced their merger
agreement - and it was paid by ALBERTSONS to Albertsons shareholders. Kroger,
the acquirer, paid no such dividend; its own next distribution was the ordinary
$0.26 quarterly dividend on 2022-11-14, which EODHD records and applies
correctly.

Arithmetic confirmation that this single record produces the entire step. For an
ex-dividend D on date t, prior prices are scaled by (1 - D / close[t-1]):

    predicted   1 - 6.85 / 42.17            = 0.8375622
    observed    0.765907 / 0.914449         = 0.8375615
    difference                                7.96e-07  (rounding of the stored
                                                         4-decimal adjusted values)

The match is essentially exact. The $6.85 record fully explains the anomaly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. COMPARISON WITH INDEPENDENT SOURCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

KR's daily return on 2022-10-21, computed four ways:

    Dataset D raw close                +3.462%
    Dataset D adjusted_close          +23.528%   <-- the only outlier
    Dataset A (Yahoo) adjusted close   +3.462%
    Official M6 price file             +3.463%

Three independent sources agree on +3.46%, including the official M6 price file
used for this project's evaluation ground truth.

Comparing the two adjusted series directly, Dataset D adjusted / Dataset A
adjusted is:

    2020-01-02 .. 2022-10-20    mean 0.83196   sd 1.1e-05
    2022-10-21 .. 2023-02-03    mean 0.99321   sd 4.8e-05

Each side is constant to five decimal places, with a single step of x1.19394 at
2022-10-21 - and 1 / 0.8375622 = 1.19394. Yahoo's series has no such step. The
two providers agree about KR's price path throughout; they disagree only about
the adjustment basis, and only across this one date.

Note what this implies: it is the PRE-2022-10-21 adjusted prices that are
depressed (by the factor 0.8376), not the later ones. Because returns are ratios
within a constant basis, a uniform level shift is harmless - only the single day
that straddles the discontinuity produces a wrong return.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. IS THERE ANY GENUINE EXPLANATION?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

No. Each possibility was checked and excluded:

  Stock split            EODHD records exactly one KR split, 2-for-1 on
                         2015-07-14, which is genuine and correctly applied. No
                         split in 2022. The raw price series shows no split-like
                         discontinuity: 42.17 -> 43.63 is a normal move.
  Special dividend       A $6.85 distribution on a $42 share is ~16% of market
                         value. Kroger did not pay one; its dividend in that
                         quarter was $0.26, which appears separately and is
                         applied correctly three weeks later.
  Spin-off or return of  None occurred. Kroger was the ACQUIRER in the
  capital                Albertsons transaction, so no capital was returned to
                         Kroger shareholders.
  Data-entry timing      The date is not merely shifted: no KR corporate action
                         of this magnitude exists anywhere nearby.
  Acquisition/alignment  Ruled out in section 1 - sorted, unique, complete,
  error on our side      internally consistent rows, single identifier, and the
                         raw fields agree with two other providers.

The evidence therefore supports one explanation only: a provider-side
misattribution of Albertsons' special dividend to Kroger's ticker in EODHD's
corporate-action feed, which propagates into their computed adjusted_close.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. WHY THIS MATTERS FOR LATER FEATURE ENGINEERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

It matters only if adjusted_close is used to build returns. Samartzis's feature
set is computed from RAW close (his data template has no adjusted-close sheet),
so a faithful re-implementation is unaffected. This project's own convention
elsewhere - Dataset A, the Stage 2 log returns, every TSFM input - is adjusted
close, so the choice must be made and recorded explicitly.

If adjusted_close were used unchanged, one corrupted daily return (2022-10-21,
+23.5% instead of +3.5%) would propagate into every window that spans it:

  affected quantity                        contaminated rows for KR
  daily return                             2022-10-21 only
  20-day / four-week return (feat_0,       2022-10-21 .. 2022-11-17
    feat_5 inputs, and any label built
    from forward four-week returns)
  40-day rolling sum (feat_3 family)       2022-10-21 .. 2022-12-16
  60-day rolling std / max / sum           2022-10-21 .. 2023-01-18
    (feat_1, feat_2, feat_4)

Two further points:

  - feat_6 (the four-week return shifted 20 x 11 = 220 business days) would
    only surface this contamination around 2023-08-25, which is beyond the
    dataset's 2023-02-03 end. Within this sample feat_6 is NOT affected.
  - 2022-10-21 falls inside M6 Round 9 (origin 2022-10-14, evaluation window
    2022-10-17 .. 2022-11-11), and the contaminated windows above also overlap
    Rounds 10 and 11. So the affected period is not a quiet corner of the
    sample - it sits inside the scored competition period.

The practical consequence would be a single asset, in three scored rounds,
carrying a spuriously large positive return and a spuriously high volatility
estimate. In a CROSS-SECTIONAL ranking task that is more damaging than it might
appear: a fabricated +23.5% move would tend to push KR toward the top quintile
of predicted or realised performance in exactly the rounds where it is scored.

Two things are NOT at risk. The evaluation ground truth is built from
Data/assets_m6.csv, which is unaffected (+3.463%). And all existing TSFM results
were produced from Dataset A, which is also unaffected. No published result in
this project is contaminated by this issue.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is an EODHD adjusted-price data error, not an acquisition or alignment
mistake on our side. The download is correct: the right security, the right
identifier, sorted unique dates, complete and internally consistent OHLCV, and
raw prices that agree with both Yahoo and the official M6 file to three decimal
places.

The single defect is that EODHD's corporate-action feed carries Albertsons'
$6.85 special dividend against Kroger's ticker on 2022-10-21, and their
adjusted_close is computed with it. This scales every KR adjusted price before
that date by 0.8376 relative to those after it, producing one fabricated +23.5%
daily return where the true move was +3.46%.

Nothing has been fixed, corrected or regenerated. The raw provider output is
preserved exactly as received, and the affected rows remain in Dataset D. The
decision about how to handle this - use raw close as Samartzis does, apply a
documented correction to adjusted close, or take the adjustment basis from
another source - belongs to the preprocessing stage, and this report is the
evidence base for making it.

Scope note: a scan of all 100 Dataset D assets found only one other asset with
any daily adjusted-close disagreement above 1% against Dataset A (COP, two days,
maximum 1.5%), consistent with ordinary dividend-timing differences between
providers rather than a comparable break. KR is the only asset with this problem.
